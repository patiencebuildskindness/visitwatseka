Welcome to Watseka, Illinois

Small Town Roots,
Big Community Heart.

Watseka is the county seat of Iroquois County, Illinois — a small town with deep roots and a big heart. Explore the charm of Downtown Watseka, from our Saturday morning Farmers Market to local shops, restaurants, and community events.

Farmers' Market

Every Saturday morning, June through October. Fresh produce, handmade goods, live music, and community — all free to attend.

Downtown Charm

Historic buildings, local businesses, and friendly faces. Downtown Watseka is the kind of place where everyone knows your name.

Community Events

From seasonal celebrations to live entertainment, there's always something happening in Watseka. Stay tuned for what's next.
