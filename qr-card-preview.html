Trail Fact No. 1
Gurdon Hubbard once walked seventy-five miles through the night to warn settlers of a threatened raid, arriving before dawn. The Potawatomi named him Pa-pa-ma-ta-be, or Swift-Walker.
The trail he wore north is now Route 1, the road just outside this door.

What this is showing

  • Each of the twenty shops would carry a different fact, so walking the whole trail collects twenty pieces of the story instead of the same card twenty times.
